# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Laravel 13 + PHP 8.3 + Filament 5 + MySQL 8.4 + Tailwind CSS v4 (per user specification).

## Users

- **Super Admin:** Group-level executive/IT/HR lead overseeing all 5 KM100 entities with global visibility and an active entity switcher.
- **HR Admin (per entity):** Entity-scoped HR practitioner managing 201 files, attendance, leave, payroll, recruitment, performance, and training for one entity.
- **Manager / Supervisor:** Department or team lead approving leave/overtime and submitting employee evaluations within their entity.
- **Employee:** Staff across the 5 KM100 entities using the self-service portal for profile, DTR, leave, and payslips.

## Product Purpose

Unified multi-entity Human Resource Information System for the KM100 Philippine corporate group (KM100 Skills and Training Center, Inc., Knoah Mari, Red Goose, STC, and PETC), supporting entity transfers with history, Philippine statutory compliance, RA 10173 Data Privacy encryption/masking, and end-to-end HR operations.

## Operating Context

High-density operational HR and payroll administration with Philippine formats (`₱` currency, `Month DD, YYYY` dates, `Asia/Manila` timezone), strict entity-level query isolation, and audit logging on sensitive changes.
